Privacy

Last updated September 22, 2026

The short version: your clips stay on your phone. To analyze an attempt, the app sends still frames from it to our server, which passes them to OpenAI and sends the breakdown back. We don't store your frames, your clips, or your chats with the coach. There are no accounts.

What leaves your phone

  • Frames from your clip. When you analyze an attempt, the app pulls up to 48 still frames from the trimmed clip and sends them with the trick name, your stance, and whether you landed it. Our server forwards them to OpenAI to produce the breakdown, then discards them.
  • Coach messages. Questions you ask the coach, plus your stance, the tricks you've landed, and a summary of your last attempt, go to OpenAI the same way and aren't stored by us.
  • A usage record. For each analysis or coach reply we keep an anonymous app user ID, the time, the AI model used, and its cost. We use this to check your subscription and prevent abuse. It contains no frames, text, or personal details.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions go through Apple. RevenueCat tells the app whether your subscription is active, using the same anonymous app user ID. We never see your payment details.
  • Usage analytics. In App Store builds, the app sends anonymous events such as "analysis completed" or "paywall shown" to PostHog so we can see which screens work. These don't include your clips or messages.

What stays on your phone

Your clips, your analyses, your trick list, and your onboarding answers. Deleting the app deletes all of it, and Settings → Delete my clips and attempts clears your clips and analyses without deleting the app.

OpenAI

We send data to OpenAI through its API with storage turned off. OpenAI doesn't use API data to train its models, and may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse before deleting it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • No location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing your data for advertising.

Kids

Skate AI is made for skaters of all ages. We don't ask for or knowingly collect personal information such as names or contact details, and nothing you film is stored on our servers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Support.

Getting a good breakdown

  • Film side-on or at a 45° angle, with your whole body and the board in frame.
  • Use slo-mo if your phone has it. The coach sees more.
  • Trim to just the attempt, 10 seconds max.
  • If it says it couldn't see you, move closer and try again.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, tap Restore on the paywall or Settings → Restore purchases.

Your data

Your clips stay on your phone. Settings → Delete my clips and attempts clears them. More in the privacy policy.

# Skate AI — AI Skateboard Coach

**Film a trick, get the fix.** Skate AI breaks down your kickflip, ollie, or any of 40 tricks frame by frame, scores every part of it, and shows exactly where your feet go on the next try.

**Website:** https://getskateai.com
**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6814983538
